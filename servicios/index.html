Frequently asked questions

What people ask us most before getting started

Is the diagnostic really free? What's the catch?

There's no catch. It's our entry point. If you decide not to hire anything after the diagnostic, you keep the proposal and work plan. It's valuable information for you and a reasonable cost for us compared with a failed project caused by not understanding the case.

Why don't you publish fixed prices for the transformation service?

Because every case is different. A conversational automation project in a clinic looks nothing like an intelligent search project in a professional firm. Fixed rates force you to inflate prices for complex cases or take losses on small ones. We prefer to quote each project after understanding it.

How soon will I see measurable results?

It depends on the project, but as a general rule between 4 and 12 weeks from kick-off. We always work in short milestones with verifiable metrics, so you see results before committing resources to a full rollout.

Is my data safe?

All our infrastructure is in the European Union (Hetzner Frankfurt and EU providers). We are GDPR compliant by default, with no third-country dependencies. If we work with regulated sectors (health, legal, finance), we add the specific safeguards your sector requires.

What happens if I want to stop working with you?

At the end of any project we hand over the code, access keys, data and documentation. Your company keeps everything that was built. The Technology partner service is reviewed quarterly and you can adjust or pause it as your company evolves.
