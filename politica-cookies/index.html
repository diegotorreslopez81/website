Cookie policy

Minimal cookies. Anonymous analytics.

This site uses strictly necessary cookies and anonymous analytics self-hosted in the EU. We do not use third-party tracking cookies or advertising.

Last updated: 16 July 2026

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device when you visit it. They are used to remember preferences, keep sessions active or measure how the site is used.

2. Which cookies do we use?

On infinitelabs.co we use a minimal number of cookies. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking cookies such as Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel or similar.

Strictly necessary cookies

Session and preferences Temporary cookies used to keep your session active (if you sign in) or to remember preferences such as language. They are automatically deleted when you close your browser or after a short period of inactivity. They do not require consent.

Anonymous analytics

Umami Analytics (self-hosted) We use Umami, an open source web analytics tool that we host ourselves in the EU. It does not use persistent cookies and does not track identities. It only counts page views in an aggregated, anonymous way.

It does not share data with third parties. As anonymous, strictly limited audience measurement, it does not require consent under the GDPR and the EU ePrivacy rules on cookies.

3. Third-party cookies

The site does not use third-party cookies for advertising or tracking. If at some point we embed external content (YouTube videos, Cal.com calendars, etc.), those services may set their own cookies. In that case, a corresponding notice will be shown.

4. How to manage cookies

You can configure your browser to accept, reject or delete cookies. Each browser offers different options:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

5. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated. Any change will be reflected here, with the date of the last update shown above.
