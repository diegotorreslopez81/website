The problem

Artificial intelligence is reshaping your industry. The question is not whether it will affect you, but how to apply it well.

Information is everywhere, but telling what truly transforms your business from what merely sounds interesting takes judgement and experience. The transformation is not only technological: it touches processes, teams and the way decisions are made.

Which repetitive tasks consume your team's day? Which decisions are made without enough data? Which areas are overloaded? What happens when a key person is away? What is the real investment to get started, and how is the return measured?

Case 2 of 5 · Industrial distribution

12 sales reps were losing two hours a day to manual orders.

Orders arrived by WhatsApp, phone and email. Sales reps spent the first two hours of every day transcribing them into the ERP.

We connected an AI assistant to the main WhatsApp line: it identifies the customer, recognises product references, validates stock and generates the order. The sales rep only steps in for exceptions.
