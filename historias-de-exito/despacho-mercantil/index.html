The starting point

A corporate law firm with 18 professionals kept drafting similar contracts over and over. Each senior partner spent 4 to 6 hours a week reviewing and drafting contracts that were, in essence, variations on five templates. Time that was not billed at premium rates and that delayed delivery to urgent clients.

The solution we deployed

We built a contract generator based on the firm's own templates. The lawyer answers a structured questionnaire and the system delivers a contract ready for review. Smart search over the historical archive retrieves precedents in seconds. Control always stays with the partner who signs.

How we did it

Eight weeks. Analysis of the five most frequent contract types, systematisation of the key variables, training on the anonymised historical archive, validation against real cases from the previous six months, and progressive rollout with calibration sessions.

The verified results

Each senior partner recovers around 20 hours a month, redirected to higher-value cases or business development. Average response time for standard contracts went from 3 days to 6 hours. End-client satisfaction improved markedly.
